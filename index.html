ENTITY-001 - THE HUSK
Containment Class: KETER

CONTAINMENT PROCEDURES

ENTITY-001 is to be kept in a 5m x 5m x 5m containment chamber lined with 30cm thick lead plating. No fewer than three armed guards must maintain visual contact with ENTITY-001 at all times. Under no circumstances should personnel enter the containment chamber.

DESCRIPTION

ENTITY-001 appears as a humanoid figure approximately 2.1 meters in height. The entity lacks skin, exposing muscle tissue that appears to be in a constant state of regeneration and decay. ENTITY-001's most notable feature is its face - or lack thereof. Where facial features should be, there is only smooth, featureless flesh.

When observed directly, ENTITY-001 remains motionless. However, surveillance footage shows the entity moves when not under direct observation. In these recordings, ENTITY-001 can be seen approaching the observation window, often placing its hands against the glass.

ADDENDUM 001-1

On ██/██/████, surveillance footage captured ENTITY-001 speaking for the first time. The entity repeated the phrase "Let me out" 47 times before returning to its default position. Audio analysis confirmed the voice matches that of Dr. ██████, who went missing during Incident 001-3.

File last updated: ██/██/████

ENTITY-002 - THE SMILING MAN
Containment Class: EUCLID

CONTAINMENT PROCEDURES

ENTITY-002 is to be contained within a standard humanoid containment cell. The cell must be illuminated at all times with no fewer than three independent light sources. Personnel entering the containment area must be equipped with night vision goggles in case of power failure.

DESCRIPTION

ENTITY-002 presents as a Caucasian male of approximately 35 years of age. The entity's most distinctive feature is its facial expression - an impossibly wide smile exposing all teeth. Despite extensive dental examination, ENTITY-002's teeth show no signs of modification to achieve this effect.

ENTITY-002 does not speak, but communicates through written notes. These notes typically contain disturbing messages about the recipient's personal life, including information the entity should have no way of knowing.

ADDENDUM 002-1

During Experiment 002-7, ENTITY-002 was placed in a room with D-8472. After 17 minutes of silence, ENTITY-002 handed D-8472 a note reading "I know what you did to your sister." D-8472 became hysterical and had to be sedated. Background checks later revealed D-8472's sister had died under suspicious circumstances.

File last updated: ██/██/████

ENTITY-003 - THE STATIC
Containment Class: EUCLID

CONTAINMENT PROCEDURES

ENTITY-003 is contained within a Faraday cage measuring 3m x 3m x 3m. No electronic devices are permitted within 10 meters of the containment area. Personnel assigned to ENTITY-003 must undergo weekly psychological evaluation.

DESCRIPTION

ENTITY-003 is an anomalous phenomenon that manifests as television static. The entity can appear on any electronic display device within a 50 meter radius of its containment chamber. When ENTITY-003 manifests, viewers report seeing humanoid shapes moving within the static.

Prolonged exposure to ENTITY-003 (more than 30 seconds) causes severe psychological effects including paranoia, auditory hallucinations, and in some cases, complete mental breakdown. Subjects often report hearing whispers coming from devices displaying ENTITY-003.

ADDENDUM 003-1

During Incident 003-4, ENTITY-003 manifested simultaneously on all monitors in Sector-7. Security footage shows personnel reacting to something within the static that wasn't visible on camera. Of the 23 personnel present, 17 committed suicide within 72 hours. The remaining 6 were found in a catatonic state, repeatedly whispering "It's in the static."

File last updated: ██/██/████
